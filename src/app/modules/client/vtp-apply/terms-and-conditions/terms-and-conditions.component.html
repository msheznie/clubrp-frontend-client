Requirements needed

Requirements for obtaining Carnet de Passage

  1. Complete the attached forms.
  2. Bank guarantee valid for one year.
  3. Photocopy of car registration.
  4. Copy of ID Card for Omanis, Passport Copy/ Resident Card for Non Omanis.
  5. NOC from ROP Traffic in case of export the vehicle by shipment.
  6. NOC from ROP Traffic in case the vehicle registration is not on your name.
  7. One Photograph.

In case of Permanent Exportation

  1. Carnet de Passage duly discharged.
  2. Certificate of location from the customs department duly endorsed by the Oman Embassy of the country the vehicle has been re-registered.
  3. Hand over both the car registration plates.
  4. Hand over the original registration card.
  5. Copy of the new vehicle registration card.

Note

In case the vehicle registration card expires before you have surrendered all the above documents, the ROP imposes a fine plus renewal fees.
So please make sure that you send all your documents before the expiry date.
